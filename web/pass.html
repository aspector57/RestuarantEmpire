The Pass

a restaurant, from the kitchen side

You have $30,000 and a decision to make. Every site takes key money before you have sold a thing, so the pitch you choose decides how much you have left to trade with — and the busiest streets leave you the least room to grow.

Nothing here is winnable by accident. A new restaurant loses money until it is big enough not to — the question is whether you can get there before the cash runs out.
